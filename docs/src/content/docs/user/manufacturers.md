Manufacturers are a short list of the companies whose products you use, such as MikroTik or Ubiquiti. Every [device model](../device-models/) belongs to one manufacturer, so add the manufacturer before its first model.

Open **Manufacturers** in the sidebar. Anyone who can sign in can view them. Creating, editing and deleting them needs the editor or administrator role.

## Details

| Field | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Name | Required and unique regardless of case: `MikroTik` and `mikrotik` are the same manufacturer. |
| Website | Optional link to the manufacturer's site. |
| Notes | Support contacts, distributor, warranty terms and anything else worth keeping. |

The **Models** tab on a manufacturer's page lists all of its device models, including decommissioned and archived ones. **Open in the model list** shows the same models in the full list, filtered to this manufacturer.

## Deleting

Delete a manufacturer only to fix a mistake, such as a duplicate or a misspelled name. It cannot be deleted while any device model is made by it. Move or delete those models first.
